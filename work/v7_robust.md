### 7c. Stage 2 without pool-size statistics (V4c), and 7d. list construction
**7c.** `q2q1`, `q2q3`, `nn_sim1`, `nn_sim_mean` describe how close a query is to the *other queries in its pool*. Their distribution depends on the pool's size and composition, which differ between a CV fold and the test set (printed below), so a model that relies on them is calibrated for the wrong pool. V4c drops them and keeps the neighbour evidence that is *verified* (SIFT inliers to neighbouring queries, the neighbours' stage-1 identity probabilities). Rule: V4c is used unless it loses more than 0.002 pooled CV MAP@5.

**7d.** For one query, MAP@5 rewards ranking the candidates by their probability of being the truth. The identity ranker outputs independent binary probabilities `p_i`, and P(new) comes from a separate model, so the two scales need not agree. `norm` turns them into one distribution: `q_i = (1 − P(new)) · p_i / Σ_j p_j`. `cap` renormalises only when `Σ p_j > 1`. A mode is used only if it beats `raw` by ≥ 0.001 pooled CV MAP@5.
